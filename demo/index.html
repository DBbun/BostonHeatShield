Boston HeatShield: heat and vulnerability map of Boston, Cambridge and Somerville

Planning demo. Public data only. Not a forecast, not real-time, and not an official city or NOAA tool.

Boston HeatShield won NOAA's 2026 Heat Tabletop Design Challenge and is being built into a largely online exercise. Want to take part, as a resident, an agency, an organization or an observer? Sign up by email The exercise is in early planning and no dates are set. I will use your details only to contact you about it. This is an independent DBbun project; no city agency or NOAA office is running it.

Sources: Census Bureau LACE 2023 (experimental estimates of homes without air conditioning, with margins of error); CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index 2022 (American Community Survey estimates); CDC PLACES (model-based small-area estimates from health surveys); City of Boston community centers and pools (Boston only so far; Cambridge and Somerville facilities are not loaded yet); MBTA GTFS schedule feed (Fall 2026 edition, scheduled routes only, no service alerts); Census TIGER 2024 tract and city boundaries; Climate Ready Boston neighborhood names (Boston only). Basemap: OpenStreetMap contributors.

All shaded values are published estimates, not exact counts. Popups show each agency's own margin of error: 90% for Census-based values (LACE, SVI), 95% confidence intervals for CDC PLACES. Small tracts have wide margins, so differences between neighboring tracts may not be meaningful. Gray tracts have no published value (mostly harbor islands, the airport, and parks). Neighborhood names marked "approx." come from a 2010 tract that overlaps the 2020 tract. Outlined neighborhoods are the exercise's scenario areas that the neighborhood source lists (Roxbury, East Boston, Dorchester); Chinatown is not a separate unit in that source.
