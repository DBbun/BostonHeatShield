Boston HeatShield: neighborhood heat and vulnerability map

Planning demo. Public data only. Not a forecast, not real-time, and not an official city or NOAA tool.

Sources: Census Bureau LACE 2023 (experimental estimates of homes without air conditioning, with margins of error); CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index 2022 (American Community Survey estimates); CDC PLACES (model-based small-area estimates from health surveys); City of Boston community centers and pools; Census TIGER 2024 tract boundaries; Climate Ready Boston neighborhood names. Basemap: OpenStreetMap contributors.

All shaded values are published estimates, not exact counts. Gray tracts have no published value (mostly harbor islands, the airport, and parks). Neighborhood names marked "approx." come from a 2010 tract that overlaps the 2020 tract. Outlined neighborhoods are the exercise's scenario areas that the neighborhood source lists (Roxbury, East Boston, Dorchester); Chinatown is not a separate unit in that source.
