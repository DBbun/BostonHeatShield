The persona swap. Four minutes. Each person steps out of their own role for two minutes and answers as a fictional person living through the heat. Go to the Cards tab and press Draw a random card, or tap each persona to read it. The characters are an older adult in a high-rise, a resident who is unhoused, a cooling site operator, a utility liaison, and a community health worker.

Ask each person: from this character's point of view, what would you need that nobody has asked about yet? Log what you hear as data gaps, by role.
