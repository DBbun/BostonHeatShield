Inject three point four, long-term investment. Ten minutes. It is Wednesday. Temperatures have fallen, but the effects continue. Money is available for only three near-term improvements.

Read the text in the blue box, which lists the options: backup power at cooling sites, microgrid planning, paid multilingual trusted-messenger work, a wellness-check protocol for high-rises, shade and cooling corridors, depaving and cool surfaces, or a heat-risk data dashboard.

Ask the group to choose three, and to say why and which equity criteria they used. For each pick, ask for an owner, a timeframe of thirty, sixty or ninety days or longer, a resource need, and the evidence it would reduce heat risk. Keep the talk on the gaps this round revealed, not on climate planning in general.
