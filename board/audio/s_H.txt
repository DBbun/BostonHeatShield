The hotwash and close. Seven minutes, and the most valuable part. Do not cut it.

Ask the four questions on screen. What worked well and should be kept? Where did the response depend on people knowing each other instead of on a written protocol? What was missing from the shared picture of the situation? And what is the one thing to fix before the next real heat wave?

Type the answers into the Notes and export tab as people speak, by role, not by name. Then read the closing words in the blue box. They thank everyone, promise a short written summary, and repeat that this is voluntary and not an official NOAA project.

Last, and do not forget this one, press Download notes before you close the page. Everything is stored only in this browser. On a shared computer, use Clear all data when you are finished.
