This is the HeatShield Facilitator Board. It helps one person run a sixty-minute online heat exercise. Everything in it is fictional, with simulated numbers. It is not an official NOAA, NIHHIS or City of Boston product, and nothing you type leaves this device.

At the top is the clock. Press the blue play button to start, and use the arrows to move between parts. If you run over, the time turns red. The thin bar underneath shows your progress through the hour.

The tabs at the bottom are Run, Risk, Log, Plan, Cards and Notes. Each one has a Listen button like this. Sound never plays by itself.

Before you begin, check that captions are on and recording is off. And download your notes at the end, because the board keeps everything only in this browser.
