This is the Cards tab. It has two sets of fictional cards.

First, five role envelopes: public health, utility liaison, community organization, housing or property manager, and transportation or paratransit. Each envelope is a private message for one participant, sent at a particular inject. The card tells you when to send it and what decision it forces. Press Copy message, then paste it into a private chat or email to that person. Show large puts the text on screen so you can read it aloud.

Second, five persona cards, used once, in the persona swap. Draw a random card, or copy one for each person.

Everything here is invented for the exercise. Do not add real names or real health details.
