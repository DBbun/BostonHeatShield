This is the Notes tab, where the session ends. Under Hotwash, tap each question and type the answers as people speak, by role, not by name. A check mark shows which ones you have answered.

Under Export, Download notes saves a text file of everything: the timer, the risk board, the decision log, the plan and the hotwash. Copy and Print are there too, and Large text makes everything bigger.

Download before you close the page. On a shared computer, clear all data afterward.
