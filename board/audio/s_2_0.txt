Module 2 begins: the worst day. Two minutes. It is Sunday afternoon, day four. Heat has been severe for days, nights stay dangerously warm, cooling sites are busy, and the utility reports local power stress. Read the blue card. Then open Scenario details for three numbers: a modeled high of one hundred four point five degrees, about eighty-seven percent of modeled areas above the thirty-six-hour heat threshold, and a heat island effect of about eleven degrees. Say out loud that these are simulated.

From here on, whenever someone says the city should do something, ask: who owns it, what authority, what data triggers it, and what if the preferred resource is not available?
