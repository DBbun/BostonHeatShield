This is the Decision log. Add one entry for each decision the group makes, using roles, not names. Type the decision, check the step, and press Add decision.

If you have time, tap Add details to record the trigger, who leads, partners, an equity check, and any data gap. The equity check is the one people skip. Ask: who benefits, and who might be missed?

Everything is saved only in this browser, and goes into your notes file when you export.
